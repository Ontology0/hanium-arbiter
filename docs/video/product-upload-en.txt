Culture-Fit | Open-source Human-in-the-Loop: Install & Get Started | elevenlabs.io

When AI agents work together, who can decide—and when should a person step in?
Culture-Fit is an open-source Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) coordination layer that connects conversational evidence, scoped authority records, and human input. Reuse it through MCP tools, an A2A analysis service, or a Skill package in your existing environment. This video introduces installation, first launch, evidence review, and handling authority conflicts with human input.

Interface: English, with no UI language selector. Conversation analysis: English and Korean. Imported text and source quotations retain their original language. Language support does not establish detection accuracy across all domains. The public demo is a web experience; configure MCP and A2A in your own installation.

The demonstration uses synthetic examples and recorded model analysis. Approval screens show local operator records, not authenticated consent from external owners or completed external work.
